CloudLab Latam — Email deliverability (anti-spam) checklist
===========================================================

Code alone cannot guarantee inbox placement. Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook require
authenticated domain mail. Do this in Hostinger DNS + mailbox:

1) Send only from your domain mailbox
   MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS=info@cloudlablatam.com
   MAIL_USERNAME=info@cloudlablatam.com
   (same domain as the website)

2) SPF (TXT on cloudlablatam.tech)
   Hostinger usually provides this automatically. Example:
   v=spf1 include:_spf.mail.hostinger.com ~all

3) DKIM
   Enable DKIM in Hostinger Email → Domain → DKIM.
   Publish the TXT record Hostinger shows.

4) DMARC (TXT on _dmarc.cloudlablatam.tech)
   v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:info@cloudlablatam.com; fo=1
   Later tighten to p=quarantine then p=reject.

5) APP_URL must be https://cloudlablatam.tech
   Tracking links (/t/o /t/c /t/u) must be publicly reachable over HTTPS.

6) Campaign best practices (built into CRM)
   - Multipart HTML + plain text
   - List-Unsubscribe header + one-click
   - Physical company footer + unsubscribe link
   - Tracked CTA button
   - Open pixel (unique opens)
   - Suppression list (unsubscribes skipped)

7) Content tips
   - Avoid ALL CAPS subjects, too many !!!, spam words
   - Prefer personalization ({{first_name}})
   - Warm up: send small batches first (CRM sends 50/click)
